Reference sites and what we borrow from each

intro.co — closest sibling pattern (verified expert marketplace + 1:1 video sessions). Borrow: tab-style category nav at top of /experts, "Verified" + 5.0 rating chip on expert cards, expert card with photo + one-line credential bio + category tags + price chip (e.g. €60 · 50 min), "How it works" 3-step (Find → Book → Meet), member quote testimonials with name + city, sticky "Book a session" CTA on profile pages, gift-a-session secondary entry point.

mavenclinic.com — evidence-based women's & family healthcare credibility tone. Borrow: serif hero with one bold-weight word for emphasis (e.g. "Care, close to you."), Programs grid with four warm cards (Pregnancy & Postpartum, Mental Health, Movement & Pelvic, Nutrition) each linking to /experts/[category], outcome stats strip ("3 languages · ERS-registered · 100% online · X verified experts"), audience-split CTA block on home (Patient / Expert / Clinic), real member-story quotes with attribution.

swordhealth.com — clinical-grade outcome storytelling. Borrow: Programs/Care lines block treated as the second hero, big-number stats grid pattern, certification badges row in footer (ERS, GDPR, SSL, ISO equivalents), editorial 3-card "Latest from Eleva" block (linkable to docs/blog when ready), footnote pattern for any clinical/outcome claim (compliance-safe per docs/eleva-v3/brand-book/README.md).

getlivo.com — two-audience clarity. Borrow: dual-audience secondary nav style, mission strip with serif quote.

hims.com + superpower.com — DTC warm-premium consumer feel. Borrow: warm sand/coral background bands between teal sections to break the page rhythm, conversational button copy ("Find my expert" instead of "Search"), photographic hero treatment with soft gradient overlay over a real person (per art-direction "real situations, natural light, diversity of age and life stage").
