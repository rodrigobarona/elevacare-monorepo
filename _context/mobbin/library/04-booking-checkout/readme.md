# Public booking funnel and checkout

> Generated from `references.json` by `docs/eleva-v3/design-audit/build.mjs`. Edit the JSON, then run the build.

Researcher seat: Booking and checkout. Date: 2026-11-14. Before captures: `docs/eleva-v3/design-audit/captures/2026-11-14-before/04-booking-checkout/`.

The funnel is short, honest and already carries a summary card, a time zone line, a dated cancellation deadline and a hold on the slot, so the bones are right. It feels weak because the money moment is thin: the pay step has no real order summary, no reassurance beside the button and a hold timer that scrolls away on a phone, while the step before it asks for five ticks and the whole thing sits inside the marketing header and a forty-link footer. The one change that matters most is to rebuild the pay step as a confirm-and-pay page: one order summary (who, what, when, price, dated cancellation), the hold and the reassurance next to the button, promo and tax number tucked into the summary, in a quiet checkout chrome. Note: capture 01-paid-funnel-entry shows the time step, because the seeded paid service has one way to meet and the funnel skips the meet step, so the meet step was not audited.

## Keep

- A summary card travels with every step and already holds expert, service, duration, way of meeting, chosen time, price and one cancellation line (01-paid-funnel-entry.desktop.jpg, 07-paid-step-pay.desktop.jpg). Keep it and make it work harder.
- The funnel is short: three steps for a paid session, and the meet step is skipped when there is only one way to meet. 'Step 1 of 3' plus a segmented bar is clear without being loud.
- The time step shows several days at once, says which time zone the times are in with a 'Change time zone' link, and the continue button repeats the choice: 'Continue with Mon, 16 Nov, 10:00' (04-paid-slot-picked.desktop.jpg). On phones that button is a fixed bar.
- The cancellation policy is turned into real dates for this booking: 'Full refund if you cancel before 15 Nov 2026, 10:00' (05-paid-step-details.desktop.jpg). That sentence is the best line in the funnel.
- Guests are never asked to create an account before booking, and the thank-you page says 'No account needed' for joining the call (09-free-thank-you.desktop.jpg).
- The pay button names the amount ('Pay €60.00'), MB WAY sits beside card for Portuguese members, and the source already has a 'Keep this time for 5 more minutes' offer when the hold is about to end.
- The thank-you page appears with a 'What happens next' list, an add-to-calendar button and the account invitation together, with no delay.
- The free path skips payment and renames the button to 'Confirm booking'.
- Headings are warm and plain ('Pick a time', 'A few details'), and the urgent-help note is a responsible thing for a health product to carry.

## Judging criteria

A review panel scores a new design for this surface against these, with the references open.

1. On every step, on desktop and on a phone, can the member see who she is booking, the service, the time with its zone, the price and one dated cancellation sentence without scrolling back? (Airbnb 04-booking-checkout/02a, Alan 04-booking-checkout/04)
2. Is the funnel free of site navigation and a marketing footer from the first step to the thank-you page, with only logo, language, help and a secure-booking mark? (GetYourGuide 04-booking-checkout/01, Airbnb 04-booking-checkout/02a)
3. Does the pay step show one order summary with a price line, any discount, the total paid today, and promo code and tax number as links inside it, and on phones a fixed bar with the total and the pay button? (Turo 04-booking-checkout/03, Fresha 04-booking-checkout/05)
4. Within one thumb's reach of the pay button, is there a security line, the dated refund promise and what happens right after paying, and is the hold shown there as a calm promise? (GetYourGuide 04-booking-checkout/01, Alan 04-booking-checkout/04)
5. Does the member make at most two ticks to book, with health data consent as its own explicit tick, the other notices as a linked sentence or readable rows, and optional choices clearly apart from required ones? (Headspace 04-booking-checkout/09a, Turo 04-booking-checkout/03)
6. On a phone, does the time step show day chips and at least six times for the chosen day on the first screen, grey out days with no times, and offer the next available time when a day or week is empty? (Future Pro 04-booking-checkout/07, Fresha 04-booking-checkout/08, Zoom 04-booking-checkout/06)
7. Does the thank-you page name the expert and the time in its headline, show the email address used, put add-to-calendar first with a choice of calendars, list dated next steps, and keep the Space invitation in its own band with a benefit and 'details already filled in'? (Headspace 04-booking-checkout/09b, Warby Parker 04-booking-checkout/10, Cal.com 04-booking-checkout/11, Oyster 04-booking-checkout/12)
8. Does checkout keep each consent to its statement and one line, with how to withdraw one tap away, and does the thank-you page say each thing once (join link, calendar, changes, Space invite)? (04-booking-checkout/20, 04-booking-checkout/21, 04-booking-checkout/22)
