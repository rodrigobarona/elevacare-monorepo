# Staff console: queues, people, approvals and logs

Researcher seat: Trust and operations. Date: 2026-11-21. Before captures: `docs/eleva-v3/design-audit/captures/2026-11-14-before/13-admin/`.

The clinic verification queue (Phase 12). References show the details to check grouped under plain labels, a short checklist where each item has its own state, and a status said in words on every row. The screens follow the licence review already shipped, so staff learn one pattern. References 04 and 06 to 16 (2026-11-21) prepare the rest of the console: finding a person and signing them out everywhere, the bar shown while acting as someone else, notes only staff see, a request that waits for a second person, a log of who did what, and failed deliveries that can be sent again. References 17 to 22 (2026-11-22) prepare two small slices: queues with pages (12.6: a count in words, Previous and Next, the page kept in the address) and the sign-in-again message (12.7: said when the page opens, with a button, and nothing typed is lost).

## Judging criteria

A review panel scores a new design for this surface against these, with the references open.

1. In the queue, can staff see which filings wait on them and which wait on the clinic, oldest first, with the clinic and its legal name on the row? (Revolut Business 13-admin/03)
2. On a filing, is everything staff must compare (legal name, NIF, billing address, website, owner) on one screen, with the public register one click from the value it checks? (Stripe 13-admin/01)
3. Is approving impossible until both checks are confirmed, and are sending back and rejecting impossible without a reason the clinic can read? (Klaviyo 13-admin/02)
4. After a decision, does the page say what happened and what happens next, and does a refusal (already decided, new details, your own clinic) say what to do?
5. On a user's page, can staff see the account state in words and every active session, and sign out one device or all of them, or suspend the account, each with a confirmation that says what will happen? (1Password 13-admin/04, Homerun 13-admin/06)
6. Does 'View as' ask for a reason before it starts, and does a bar that names the person and offers 'Stop viewing' stay on every page while it lasts? (Clerk 13-admin/07, Base44 13-admin/08)
7. Is it clear at the composer that a note is for Eleva staff only, does each note show who wrote it and when, and is the note absent from everything the member, the expert or the clinic can open (their activity, exports and emails)? (Shopify 13-admin/09, Zendesk 13-admin/10)
8. For an action that needs two people, can the second person see what is asked, by whom, when and why, and approve or reject it, while the first sees that it is waiting and for how many approvals, and is the person who asked unable to approve their own request? (Airwallex 13-admin/11, Acctual 13-admin/12)
9. Can staff answer who did what, to which record and when from the audit log, by filtering on person, action, record and date, with each row readable as a sentence? (Front 13-admin/13, Customer.io 13-admin/14)
10. Can staff find the webhook events that failed, see why each one failed and how many times it was tried, run one or several again, and then see the result of each one, not only that a retry was asked? (Resend 13-admin/15, Customer.io 13-admin/16)
11. Does each queue load one page at a time, say where the reader is in words ('Showing 26 to 50 of 112'), move with 'Previous' and 'Next' buttons that are disabled at the ends, and keep the page in the address so Back, reload and a shared link open the same rows? (Navattic 13-admin/17, StackAI 13-admin/18, Circle 13-admin/19)
12. When a decision needs a newer sign-in, does the reviewer learn it when the page opens, before typing; does the message say why and offer 'Sign in again'; and are the ticks and the typed reason still there after signing in, with no detail asked twice? (GitBook 13-admin/20, Todoist 13-admin/21, Airwallex 13-admin/22)
