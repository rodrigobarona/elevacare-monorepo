# Member Space

> Generated from `references.json` by `docs/eleva-v3/design-audit/build.mjs`. Edit the JSON, then run the build.

Researcher seat: Dashboards. Date: 2026-11-14. Before captures: `docs/eleva-v3/design-audit/captures/2026-11-14-before/10-member-space/`.

The member Space is tidy and on brand, and its privacy controls are complete, but on first run it reads as an empty admin panel: home, sessions, reports, payments and inbox are each a dashed box saying nothing is here yet. Nothing welcomes a new member, helps her choose an expert, or explains how a session works. The one change that matters most is to turn home into a companion page: a next-session card with her expert, Join and what to prepare when something is booked, and a guided way to find the right expert when nothing is.

## Keep

- The visual language is calm and consistent with the rest of Eleva: Lora headings, generous cards, one teal primary action per block.
- Every empty state on home and Sessions offers 'Find an expert', so there is always a way forward.
- Privacy is complete and honest: consents, data download and account deletion are on one page, and deletion explains the 14-day waiting period and that it can be cancelled.
- The join rule is already written in plain words in the product copy ('You can join video sessions from 15 minutes before the start. Sessions are not recorded.'); it deserves a more prominent place.
- Settings covers what a member needs in one place: name, time zone, language, theme, notification channels and quiet hours.
- The sidebar is short (six items) and uses words a member understands: Sessions, Payments, Reports, Privacy.

## Judging criteria

A review panel scores a new design for this surface against these, with the references open.

1. With nothing booked, does home welcome her by name and offer at least two specific ways to find the right expert (by need, suggested experts), instead of empty boxes? (10-member-space/02, 10-member-space/04)
2. With a session booked, does home lead with one card showing her expert's photo and name, the time in her time zone, and a Join button that states when it opens? (10-member-space/01, 10-member-space/06)
3. Does that card tell her what to do before the session and let her add it to her calendar or reschedule without leaving home? (10-member-space/01, 10-member-space/03)
4. Does Sessions show one list with Upcoming and Past tabs, and does the session detail put who, what, how to meet and the cancel rule on one screen? (10-member-space/05, 10-member-space/07)
5. Does every empty state say what will appear, when, and offer one action? (10-member-space/11, 10-member-space/04)
6. On Privacy, can she tell at a glance what she agreed to and which optional choices are on or off, and does a data request show its status? (10-member-space/08, 10-member-space/09)
7. On a 390 px screen, do notification settings fit without sideways scrolling, with essential session messages marked as always on? (10-member-space/10)
