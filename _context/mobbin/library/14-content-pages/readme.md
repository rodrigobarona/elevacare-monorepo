## Judging criteria

A review panel scores a new design for this surface against these, with the references open.

1. Is the hero a heading of eight words or fewer and one lead of 25 words or fewer, with nothing else before the first action? (14-content-pages/01 Calm, 14-content-pages/12 Metic)
2. Does each section hold one idea under a heading that says it, in two short paragraphs or fewer, and on /history do the headings tell the story when read alone? (14-content-pages/15 Runway, 14-content-pages/09 Dovetail)
3. Does every button and link name what happens next ('Subscribe', 'Listen', 'Read the issue', 'Send message'), with no 'Read more', 'Learn more', 'Submit', 'Get started' or 'here'? (14-content-pages/02 Tash, 14-content-pages/05 Maze, 14-content-pages/12 Metic)
4. Is each fact said once on the page: no heading repeated in the first line under it, and no promise made in the hero and again in the body? (14-content-pages/07 Dropbox, 14-content-pages/17 Zendesk)
5. Is the page free of internal and legal words (platform integrity, clinical artefacts, exercising your rights under the GDPR), is a vendor named only where the reader will land on its site, and are readers called members? (14-content-pages/09 Dovetail, 14-content-pages/06 TheyDo)
6. Does the newsletter sign-up ask only for an email, and say beside the field what arrives and how often? (14-content-pages/01 Calm, 14-content-pages/02 Tash)
7. Does the newsletter, podcast or community page show at least one real issue, episode or topic with its date or length, or, when there is none yet, say what will appear there and offer one action? (14-content-pages/03 Webflow, 14-content-pages/04 Front, 14-content-pages/05 Maze, 14-content-pages/16 Airbnb)
8. Does /contact give a reply time in plain words, and one clear route for a reader who is not sure who to write to? (14-content-pages/12 Metic, 14-content-pages/11 Tines, 14-content-pages/13 Maxima Therapy)
