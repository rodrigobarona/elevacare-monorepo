# Expert onboarding: from a new account to a working practice

> Generated from `references.json` by `docs/eleva-v3/design-audit/build.mjs`. Edit the JSON, then run the build.

Researcher seat: Onboarding and activation. Date: 2026-11-14. Before captures: `docs/eleva-v3/design-audit/captures/2026-11-14-before/06-expert-onboarding/`.

The wizard is calm and honest, but it is an admin form: it opens with a tax number, never asks who the expert is, and never shows her the profile she is building. It also sits inside a dashboard whose every link sends her back to the form, and a new account that came to offer care lands on a member Space with no visible way in. The change that matters most is to rebuild setup as one focused, full-screen journey in profile-first order (about you, how you see members, first service, licence, payouts, invoices) with a live profile preview, then open the dashboard with a checklist.

## Keep

- The finish step says exactly what happens next in plain words: 'When you finish, we check your licence with your Ordem. Members can book you once it's verified.' Keep this honesty (06-setup-step-5).
- The licence intro is good trust copy: 'Eleva lists regulated health professionals only. We check these details with your professional body before members can find you.' The 'Why we ask' disclosures are the right instinct (06-setup-step-2).
- The Online / In person / Phone cards are large, tappable and have a sensible default; they work well on mobile (06-setup-step-1.mobile).
- The tax number is optional ('You can add it later') and Portugal is preselected and locked in the country list, so nothing blocks a Portuguese expert early.
- Stripe onboarding is embedded in the page, so the expert never leaves Eleva. The engineering choice is right; only the words around it are weak.
- The cream, teal and Lora system holds together on every step, and the desktop step rail is easy to read.
- A hook already exists: the avatar menu offers a shortcut to create an expert workspace when the account has none (packages/dashboard/src/account-menu-model.ts). It only needs to be visible.

## Judging criteria

A review panel scores a new design for this surface against these, with the references open.

1. Can a person who signed up to offer care reach expert setup from her first screen without opening a menu? (06-expert-onboarding/01, 06-expert-onboarding/02)
2. Does setup run without dashboard navigation, with Save and exit, a help link and one progress indicator from the first question to 'send for review'? (06-expert-onboarding/01, 06-expert-onboarding/09)
3. Does every screen ask for one thing, with a line saying why it is asked, and a primary button within thumb reach on a 390 px screen? (06-expert-onboarding/01, 06-expert-onboarding/12)
4. Do the first three steps build the public profile (who she is, how she sees members, a first service), with tax, payouts and invoices after them? (06-expert-onboarding/01, 06-expert-onboarding/05)
5. Can she see her profile card update while she answers, and preview the full profile before sending it? (06-expert-onboarding/04, 06-expert-onboarding/09)
6. Before any bank or identity form, does the screen name Stripe, list what she needs, and say when and how much she is paid, with a way to do it later? (06-expert-onboarding/05, 06-expert-onboarding/12)
7. After she sends her licence, does she see a status with an expected wait, and a Today page whose next steps are real links tied to an outcome? (06-expert-onboarding/08, 06-expert-onboarding/10, 06-expert-onboarding/11)
