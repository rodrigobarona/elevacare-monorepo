# Marketing pages: become an expert, for clinics, about

> Generated from `references.json` by `docs/eleva-v3/design-audit/build.mjs`. Edit the JSON, then run the build.

Researcher seat: Marketing and brand. Date: 2026-11-14. Before captures: `docs/eleva-v3/design-audit/captures/2026-11-14-before/08-marketing/`.

All three pages are tidy and honest, but the two pages for professionals describe the platform in careful, contract-like language and never show the product, a price block or a peer. The clinics page tells the reader three times that terms come later while asking her to create an account. The change that matters most: rebuild /become-expert around the offer an expert cares about (15% per booking and nothing else, your prices and policies, paid through Stripe), shown with real screens and a worked example.

## Keep

- The fee and payout timing are stated in exact figures on the page (15% of each booking, no other deductions, 7 days after payment or 24 hours after the session). That honesty is rare and worth making louder.
- Concrete product facts are already written: Flexible, Moderate or Strict cancellation per service, calendar sync, 'Sessions are not recorded', registration checked before a profile goes live.
- The closing note on /become-expert (terms are the Expert agreement, 15 days' notice of changes, you can leave before a change applies) builds trust with a careful professional audience.
- About shows real people: two named founders with photos and eight advisors with credentials and a quote each.
- The four numbered steps on both professional pages are short, in order, and say what actually happens.
- Visual system is consistent with the homepage: same type, palette, radius and section rhythm.

## Judging criteria

A review panel scores a new design for this surface against these, with the references open.

1. Does the /become-expert hero state the benefit, the fee and who sets prices before any scrolling, in under 40 words? (08-marketing/01 Fiverr)
2. Is the fee shown as its own block with what it includes and a worked example or calculator in euros? (08-marketing/04 Gumroad, 08-marketing/05 Eventbrite, 08-marketing/06 Contra)
3. Does each professional page show at least three real Eleva screens tied to named features? (08-marketing/03 Ease)
4. Can a clinic start a conversation from /for-clinics through a short form, with the page saying what happens next and nothing about what is not ready? (08-marketing/09 Notion, 08-marketing/02 Ease)
5. Does at least one named peer (profession, city, own words) appear on the pages that recruit professionals? (08-marketing/07 Webflow)
6. Does an FAQ answer cost, payout timing, cancellations and leaving in the expert's own questions? (08-marketing/08 Square)
7. Does About open with the founders' own words, and is every large number on the page real evidence? (08-marketing/10 Daylight, 08-marketing/11 Function)
8. Is the /become-expert hero a headline of eight words or fewer and a lead of 20 words or fewer, with the fee stated in six words or fewer? (08-marketing/12 ClassPass, 08-marketing/14 Wise)
9. Does the fee block show a worked example as rows a reader can check without arithmetic: member pays, Eleva keeps, you receive? (08-marketing/13 Unfair)
10. Is every FAQ question in the expert's own words, with 'I' or 'my', and does every answer give the answer in its first sentence? (08-marketing/15 Railway, 08-marketing/16 Acctual)
11. Does every product screen have a title of three words or fewer and one caption of eight words or fewer? (08-marketing/17 ISO Meet)
12. Does every button on the three pages name what happens next, with no 'Learn more', 'Get started' or 'Submit', and does the /for-clinics hero have one action that names a conversation? (08-marketing/19 Employment Hero, 08-marketing/22 Mailchimp)
13. Is each fact said once per page: the fee, payout timing, who provides the care, and how clinic terms are agreed? (08-marketing/14 Wise, 08-marketing/21 Headspace)
14. Is the founders' note on About 120 words or fewer, in the first person, signed by name, and readable whole on a phone without a 'show more'? (08-marketing/20 KÖPPEN)
15. Are all three pages free of product and contract words (Team organization, modality, seat pricing, standard plan, operational owner), and is every sentence in the hero and fee block 20 words or fewer? (08-marketing/16 Acctual, 08-marketing/14 Wise)
16. Does About end with the member's next step first, or equal to the expert's? (08-marketing/22 Mailchimp)
