# Expert explorer (/experts)

> Generated from `references.json` by `docs/eleva-v3/design-audit/build.mjs`. Edit the JSON, then run the build.

Researcher seat: Marketplace and profile. Date: 2026-11-14. Before captures: `docs/eleva-v3/design-audit/captures/2026-11-14-before/02-discovery/`.

The explorer is a warm heading over a grid of cards that say only a name, one free-text line and a price. A member cannot start from her need, cannot compare two experts without opening both, and sees three blank monogram panels where people should be. The change that matters most is the card: give it a face (or a compact layout when there is none), a profession and focus, languages, how and where sessions happen, the next open time and a checked-registration mark, then put areas of care above the grid as the way in.

## Keep

- The heading and lead are in the right voice: "Real people with real training. Pick someone whose work fits you, then meet by video, by phone or in person." Keep them.
- On phones the card turns into a compact row (small portrait left, text right), so a short list fits in one screen (01-experts.mobile.jpg).
- Ordering is disclosed. "How we order experts" links to the ranking terms, and the source labels the paid plan honestly ("Top Expert · paid plan", "Top Expert first (paid plan)"). Few marketplaces are this open; make it more visible rather than changing it.
- The trust line has the right content: "Before a profile goes live, our team checks the expert's professional registration."
- The whole card is one link and one tab stop, and the free option is said in plain words ("Free session available").
- The source already has a first-run state for an empty directory with three next steps (newsletter, how Eleva works, are you an expert). The pattern is right; it only needs a design pass.

## Judging criteria

A review panel scores a new design for this surface against these, with the references open.

1. Can a member narrow the list by area of care, language and how she meets without opening a profile, and do areas with nobody yet show as "Opening soon" instead of disappearing? (02-discovery/04, 02-discovery/03)
2. Does every card state profession, focus, languages, how you meet, price with session length and the next open time? (02-discovery/01, 02-discovery/02, 02-discovery/09)
3. Does a card with no portrait still look finished, with no large empty panel? (02-discovery/06)
4. Is checked registration visible on each card and explained in one sentence above the grid? (02-discovery/05, 02-discovery/07)
5. Is the current order stated next to the result count, and is paid placement labelled on the card on phones as well as desktop? (02-discovery/11, 02-discovery/07)
6. When filters leave one or zero experts, can the member remove a single filter from the results area and see what else is close? (02-discovery/08, 02-discovery/10)
7. On a phone, do filters open in one sheet with a sticky button that shows how many experts will match? (02-discovery/07, 02-discovery/03)
8. Do the next open time, how you meet and the price sit in the same place on every card, each with a label, and does no value wrap onto a second line at 390px? (02-discovery/14, 02-discovery/02)
9. Do the portraits in the grid share one crop ratio and face position, so no card looks heavier than its neighbour? (02-discovery/13)
10. On a phone, do the filter chips and the first expert both show on the first screen, and does a list of one or two end with help for the member? (02-discovery/18, 02-discovery/15)
