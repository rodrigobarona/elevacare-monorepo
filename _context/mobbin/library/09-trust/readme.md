# Trust, legal and governance (cross-cutting)

> Generated from `references.json` by `docs/eleva-v3/design-audit/build.mjs`. Edit the JSON, then run the build.

Researcher seat: Trust, legal and governance. Date: 2026-11-14. Before captures: `docs/eleva-v3/design-audit/captures/2026-11-14-before/09-trust/`.

The legal substance is unusually complete for a pre-launch product: consents are separate and unticked, every policy is dated, the controller is named, and export and deletion are in the product. The design does not yet let a member feel that. Trust facts sit in grey fine print, in documents and in FAQ accordions, while the moments that carry the most weight (the health-data consent, the pay step, the privacy controls) are the least explained. The one change that matters most: explain each trust moment in one plain sentence at the place where it is asked, starting with the health-data consent and the cancellation deadline next to the Pay button. References 13 and 14 (2026-11-21) prepare planned maintenance: a notice in advance with the exact window, and a status page that answers in one line. References 15 to 17 (2026-11-22) prepare the dashboard error page (slice 13.2): the error stays inside the app, 'Try again' works, there is a second way out and a reference to copy for support. References 18 to 33 (2026-11-23) prepare the trust centre and policy layout (slice D6.1) and the trust moments of the public site: the plain-language layer and contents on phones, acting on a right from the page that describes it, the cookie dialog, newsletter consent, who runs Eleva in the footer and on the contact page, the pay step and the accessibility statement.

## Keep

- Checkout consents are four separate, unticked checkboxes, and the AI and transcription choice is marked Optional and left off (04-booking-checkout/05-paid-step-details). Keep the separation; improve the explanation.
- The details step turns the cancellation policy into dated facts for this booking: "Full refund if you cancel before 15 Nov 2026, 10:00" (04-booking-checkout/06-paid-details-filled). This is the best trust line in the funnel.
- Every card on the trust index has a one-line plain description and a last-updated date, and the page ends with privacy@ and security@ contacts plus a vulnerability disclosure page (09-trust/01-trust).
- The privacy notice has a last-updated date, numbered sections, a sticky contents list on desktop, a purposes and legal bases table, the controller's legal name, NIPC and address, and the route to complain to the CNPD (09-trust/02-legal-privacy).
- Consents, data export and account deletion live on one Privacy page in the member sidebar, deletion has a 14-day waiting period, and marketing notifications are off by default (10-member-space/05-privacy, 10-member-space/06-settings).
- The "Need urgent help?" block with 112, SNS 24 and the crisis lines appears where a health note is typed and again on the thank-you page (04-booking-checkout/09-free-thank-you). It reads as care, not as a disclaimer.
- The product already says who provides care: the hero line "Eleva connects you with independent health experts who run their own practice", the profile questions "Who provides my care?" and "What does Eleva check?", and the registration-check line on the experts page.
- The profile shows the professional body, licence number and ERS registration number, and the footer links the electronic complaints book and reopens cookie settings.

## Judging criteria

A review panel scores a new design for this surface against these, with the references open.

1. Does the health-information consent explain, on the same screen and in one or two plain sentences, what is processed, who sees it and how to withdraw, while staying a separate unticked choice? (09-trust/01, 09-trust/02)
2. On the screen with the Pay button, at phone width, can you read the dated cancellation deadline, the total as one row, and a line agreeing to the terms, without scrolling away from the button? (09-trust/03)
3. Does the pay step name the independent expert as the provider of the session and state Eleva's role in booking and payment? (09-trust/04)
4. Does the expert profile show the professional body and registration number as a checked fact next to the name or the booking CTA, with a one-tap explanation of what Eleva checks? (09-trust/05)
5. Does the trust centre state at least five specific facts on the page itself before listing any document, and say who runs Eleva? (09-trust/06, 09-trust/12)
6. Can a member see the current state and date of every consent, change each optional one with a single switch, and follow an export or deletion request through a dated status, with a delete confirmation that names what is erased and what is kept? (09-trust/08, 09-trust/09, 09-trust/10)
7. Can a reader of any policy page get a short summary, jump between sections on a phone, start a data request from the rights section, and report a concern about an expert in two steps with a reference number? (09-trust/07, 09-trust/11)
8. Before and during planned maintenance, does Eleva say when it starts and ends in the reader's time zone and what will not work, and does the status page answer whether Eleva is working in one sentence? (09-trust/13, 09-trust/14)
9. When a signed-in page fails to load, does the menu stay usable, does the page say in plain words what happened and what to do, does 'Try again' load the page again, is there a second way out, and can the reader copy a reference to send to support? (09-trust/15, 09-trust/16, 09-trust/17)
10. Does the trust centre group its content by what a visitor asks (health information, security, expert checks, documents), answer at least four questions in a member's words on the page, and end with one contact block? (09-trust/18)
11. Does every policy page open with an 'In short' summary set apart from the legal text, show a last-updated date with a link to the previous version, and name the controller in the first section? (09-trust/19, 09-trust/20, 09-trust/23)
12. At 390px wide, does a policy page show its contents list within the first two screens, let the reader move to a sibling policy in one tap, and keep every link at least 24px tall? (09-trust/21, 09-trust/22)
13. Is there a button to act on a right in the place where the right is described (cookie settings, download your data, delete your account), with the time to a reply stated, and a one-field route for people who booked without an account? (09-trust/24, 09-trust/26, 09-trust/27)
14. In the cookie dialog, can you read what each group does without opening it, is the essential group marked 'Always on' in words, and are reject and accept the same size and style on the first layer? (09-trust/25, 09-trust/08)
15. Does the newsletter sign-up ask for an email address only, state beside the form what will be sent and how to unsubscribe, and stay separate from any other agreement? (09-trust/28)
16. Does the footer separate trust links from legal links and state the legal name, registration number and registered address, and does /trust or /contact show the same identity as labelled facts with an address for each purpose? (09-trust/29, 09-trust/30, 09-trust/32)
17. On the pay screen, can you read the total with its tax note, one payment-security line that names the payment provider, and links to the terms and the cancellation policy, all without scrolling away from the button? (09-trust/31)
18. Does the accessibility statement show its date, name the standard it works to, list what it covers and give a way to report a barrier? (09-trust/33)
