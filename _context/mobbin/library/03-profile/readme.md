# Expert public profile (/{username})

> Generated from `references.json` by `docs/eleva-v3/design-audit/build.mjs`. Edit the JSON, then run the build.

Researcher seat: Marketplace and profile. Date: 2026-11-14. Before captures: `docs/eleva-v3/design-audit/captures/2026-11-14-before/03-profile/`.

The profile's booking mechanics are sound: real next times, a sticky card on desktop, a sticky bar on phones, a cancellation line on every service. What is weak is the person. With no photo, no profession line and a short bio, the page is a monogram, a name and grey small print, and the one fact Eleva has checked (the professional registration) reads like a legal footnote. The change that matters most is to turn that checked registration into a visible proof block beside the name and to give every profile a structured "about" (profession, focus, approach, training, what a first session looks like) that still looks complete when an expert is new.

## Keep

- Next open times are on the profile itself, grouped by day, with the member's timezone named. On desktop the booking card stays in view while she reads (01-profile-paid.desktop.jpg).
- Phones already have a sticky bar with the starting price and "See times" (01-profile-paid.mobile.jpg, 02-profile-free.mobile.jpg). Keep it and make it do more.
- The registration line is real, checkable proof: the professional body links to its public register and the licence number is shown. Few marketplaces offer this. It needs presence, not replacement.
- Every service states its cancellation window in plain words ("Free cancellation up to 24 hours before"), and the booking card closes with "No account needed".
- "How I can help" and "About me" are in the expert's own voice, which suits a page about a person.
- The FAQ asks the right questions ("Who provides my care?", "What does Eleva check?") and answers them honestly.
- The source already supports a large portrait hero, area-of-care links above the name, a credential line and a "Training and credentials" timeline. They are simply empty for a new expert, so the design problem is the fallback, not the full state.

## Judging criteria

A review panel scores a new design for this surface against these, with the references open.

1. Is the checked registration a visible block beside the name that says who checked, what and when, with a one-tap explanation? (03-profile/07, 03-profile/04)
2. Does the first phone screen show who she is (photo, name, profession) and when she is next free? (03-profile/01)
3. Can a member start booking from anywhere on a phone through one sticky action that opens the picker? (03-profile/09, 03-profile/05)
4. Does each time appear once, with service and how or where chosen before the time, across a strip of days? (03-profile/01, 03-profile/10)
5. Does the profile have fixed, labelled sections for approach, training and a three-step first session, in the same place on every profile? (03-profile/03, 03-profile/11)
6. Does a new expert's page (no reviews, short bio) still look complete and ordered? (03-profile/12, 03-profile/06)
7. When an expert cannot be booked, does the booking block explain why and offer a next step? (03-profile/02)
