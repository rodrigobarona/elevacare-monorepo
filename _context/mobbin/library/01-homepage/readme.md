# Public homepage

> Generated from `references.json` by `docs/eleva-v3/design-audit/build.mjs`. Edit the JSON, then run the build.

Researcher seat: Marketing and brand. Date: 2026-11-14. Before captures: `docs/eleva-v3/design-audit/captures/2026-11-14-before/01-homepage/`.

The homepage looks calm and well made, and the headline says what Eleva is in one line, but it never shows a member a single expert they can book: the only named faces are advisors, and the route to a real expert is one generic button. Proof is thin (four two-word labels, no member voice, no numbers) and the page ends on a pitch to experts. The change that matters most is to put the choice of need and a row of real, bookable experts in the first two screens, and move the advisors below them as supporting proof.

## Keep

- The headline 'Women's health experts, on your schedule.' plus the lead line answers what Eleva is and who it is for in under ten seconds. Keep both.
- Lora headings on cream with deep teal read as calm and premium, not clinical. The type scale and spacing are consistent from section to section.
- The board of advisors is real proof: named people with credentials and a quote. Few health marketplaces at this stage have that.
- 'No waiting lists and no account needed to book' is a strong, concrete promise, and the three steps are written in plain words.
- One label, 'Find an expert', is used in the header, the hero and on mobile, so the main action is never renamed.
- The 'Grow your practice with Eleva' band is clearly for a different audience: its own block, its own coral button, at the end of the page.

## Judging criteria

A review panel scores a new design for this surface against these, with the references open.

1. Can a first-time visitor choose a need or type a search inside the hero, without scrolling? (01-homepage/01 Fresha, 01-homepage/02 Hims)
2. Does at least one real, bookable expert card (photo or fallback, profession, languages, price from, next slot) appear within the first two screens on a phone? (01-homepage/03 Zocdoc)
3. Does the expert card still look complete when the expert has no photo and a two-line bio? (01-homepage/04 Glide)
4. Does the page state that every expert's professional registration is checked, with at least one true number beside it? (01-homepage/05 Hims)
5. Do the advisors appear once, in a single band with credentials and institutions, placed after the experts and services? (01-homepage/06 Superpower, 01-homepage/07 SwimClub)
6. Does each 'how it works' step show a piece of the real Eleva interface? (01-homepage/09 Function)
7. Does the page answer 'what is Eleva, who are the experts, what does it cost' in an FAQ and end with a call to action for members before the expert band? (01-homepage/10 Hims)
