# Expert practice dashboard

> Generated from `references.json` by `docs/eleva-v3/design-audit/build.mjs`. Edit the JSON, then run the build.

Researcher seat: Dashboards. Date: 2026-11-14. Before captures: `docs/eleva-v3/design-audit/captures/2026-11-14-before/07-expert-dashboard/`.

The expert app is visually calm and on brand, and its editors (schedule, public profile) are clear, but the home page is a counter row plus a list of links that repeat the sidebar. It does not tell an expert what to do today: there is no join action on the next session, no earnings, no share-your-profile prompt, and it stays silent while Finance says payouts are not set up. The one change that matters most is to rebuild Today around a next-session card with Join, a short 'needs you' list (requests, payouts, missing photo) and money at a glance.

## Keep

- Palette, Lora headings and DM Sans body are applied consistently across all ten screens; the app already feels like Eleva, not a generic admin template.
- The public profile editor teaches while you fill it in: the photo card's 'Works well / Avoid' lists and the 'A bio that helps members choose' guide (05-profile) are the best first-run help in the product and should be the model for other screens.
- The Finance empty state (07-finance) says what to do, why, how long it takes and that it stays on Eleva. Keep this copy.
- Weekly hours (04-schedule) are easy to read: one row per day, a checkbox to switch a day off, 'Unavailable' in words, and a plain helper line.
- The mobile calendar (02-calendar.mobile) switches from a grid to an agenda list with mode icon, time range and service. That is the right mobile pattern.
- The source already has a 'Waiting for your answer' block with a respond-by date and a licence verification notice on home; the page has the right instincts, it just does not show enough of them.
- Service rows show a clear Published or Draft pill, price, duration and languages on one line (03-event-types).

## Judging criteria

A review panel scores a new design for this surface against these, with the references open.

1. Within the first screen of Today, on desktop and mobile, can the expert see her next session and join it with one tap, with a countdown and the join window explained? (07-expert-dashboard/02, 07-expert-dashboard/05)
2. Does Today list what needs her (requests waiting, payouts not connected, profile gaps) from real state, each with a reason, a time estimate and one action, and does it disappear when done? (07-expert-dashboard/01, 07-expert-dashboard/04)
3. Is money visible on Today even at €0 (earned this month, next payout), and is an unconnected payout account impossible to miss? (07-expert-dashboard/03, 07-expert-dashboard/12)
4. Can she copy and share her public profile link from Today and from each service row without opening a menu? (07-expert-dashboard/03, 07-expert-dashboard/08)
5. Does every booking use one status vocabulary with a distinct look per state across Today, Calendar and Members, and one time format per locale? (07-expert-dashboard/06, 07-expert-dashboard/05)
6. On mobile, does the calendar always show today, let her move day by day with a week strip, and keep each schedule day on one line? (07-expert-dashboard/07, 07-expert-dashboard/10)
7. Is the service editor organised in named sections an expert understands, with visibility and save always reachable and no wrapped tabs on a 390 px screen? (07-expert-dashboard/09)
8. Within 5 seconds, can an expert say which calendar new sessions go to, without scrolling? (07-expert-dashboard/13, 07-expert-dashboard/14)
9. After connecting, is the primary calendar already checked for conflicts and set as the destination without any action, and does a message say so? (07-expert-dashboard/15, 07-expert-dashboard/18)
10. Before leaving Eleva for Google or Microsoft, does the expert read in plain words what Eleva will see and do, and what it will not do? (07-expert-dashboard/19, 07-expert-dashboard/20)
11. Is every account shown by its email and provider logo, and can she tell two calendars with the same name apart? (07-expert-dashboard/21, 07-expert-dashboard/16)
12. Does each calendar row do one job, with the destination chosen in one place and only from calendars Eleva can write to? (07-expert-dashboard/13, 07-expert-dashboard/17)
13. Does Reconnect appear only on an account that needs it, with one line on what stopped working and what it means for her bookings? (07-expert-dashboard/22, 07-expert-dashboard/23)
14. With no calendar connected, does the page explain the two things a connection does and offer one clear way to add one? (07-expert-dashboard/24, 07-expert-dashboard/19)
15. On a 390 px screen, is every calendar one line (name, badge, switch), with the destination select above the account cards and no sideways scroll? (07-expert-dashboard/17)
16. After setup, can an expert change every answer from setup (except the licence) on a settings page, and does a refused change say which services it affects and where to fix them? (07-expert-dashboard/25, 07-expert-dashboard/26, 07-expert-dashboard/27)
17. Can the expert set when her calendar reminds her of a session, next to where sessions go, with her calendar app's usual reminder as the default and a line that says members are reminded separately? (07-expert-dashboard/29, 07-expert-dashboard/30, 07-expert-dashboard/31)
18. Can she see every calendar of an account and which ones are checked without opening anything, with the calendar sessions go to clearly switched on? (07-expert-dashboard/13, 07-expert-dashboard/31)
19. Does each page open with its headline (no label above it) and one line on what you can do there? (07-expert-dashboard/32)
20. Does Finance show only what works today, in plain words? Only the ways of invoicing an expert can use are offered; an invoice row is named after its session, with one plain status and one action; nothing mentions tokens, hashes, error codes or what is not switched on. (07-expert-dashboard/33, 07-expert-dashboard/34, 07-expert-dashboard/35, 07-expert-dashboard/36, 07-expert-dashboard/37)
