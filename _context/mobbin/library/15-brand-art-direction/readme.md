# Brand and art direction for the public site

> Generated from `references.json` by `docs/eleva-v3/design-audit/build.mjs`. Edit the JSON, then run the build.

Researcher seat: Brand and art direction. Date: 2026-11-23. Before captures: `docs/eleva-v3/design-audit/captures/2026-11-14-before/15-brand-art-direction/`.

The public site is tidy, honest and calm, and it could belong to anyone. Deep teal does every job, the photographs come from many shoots with no shared treatment, the serif never gets large enough to show character, and every section is the same row of rounded cards. The references here show how health and wellness brands build a recognisable identity from a few decisions made firmly: a palette with a role for each colour, one photographic treatment, one display moment per page, a small set of shapes, and a signature ending. Three worked directions with palettes, type and contrast figures are in the panel's brand-directions.md. The change that matters most, whichever direction is chosen: give each colour one job and put every photograph through one treatment.

## Keep

- Cream paper with deep teal already reads calm and trustworthy, and text contrast is strong: ink on the page is 16 to 1, teal on the page 5.8 to 1 and the button label 5.7 to 1, by calculation.
- Fraunces with DM Sans is a sound pair, already self-hosted, and the tokens hold fluid display steps and warm shadows that a new direction can reuse.
- The large rounded portrait on the expert profile is the most distinctive picture on the site, and the deep teal quote band shows that one strong field works.
- The leaf mark and wordmark are recognisable, and the mono eyebrow gives pages a quiet structure worth keeping.

## Judging criteria

A review panel scores a new design for this surface against these, with the references open.

1. Does every colour on the page have one visible job (page, surface, text, action, emphasis, deep field, trust tint), with the action colour on nothing that cannot be pressed? (15-brand-art-direction/03 Function, 15-brand-art-direction/22 Monarch, 15-brand-art-direction/18 Ragged Edge)
2. Does every piece of text reach 4.5 to 1 against its background, including accent-coloured words and the label on the primary button, checked by calculation and not by eye? (15-brand-art-direction/04 Hims and 15-brand-art-direction/22 Monarch show pairs to avoid)
3. Do all photographs on the page share one treatment: the same light, crop and grade, or the same cut-out or wash? (15-brand-art-direction/06 Hims, 15-brand-art-direction/10 Miro, 15-brand-art-direction/11 Until, 15-brand-art-direction/12 Anima)
4. Is there exactly one display-size moment per page (8% of the viewport width or more on desktop, 12% or more on a phone), with body text at 17px or more and lines of 45 to 80 characters? (15-brand-art-direction/01 Oura, 15-brand-art-direction/13 Amperos, 15-brand-art-direction/20 Craft Agency)
5. Is emphasis (italic or accent colour) used on one phrase per page at most? (15-brand-art-direction/01 Oura, 15-brand-art-direction/04 Hims)
6. Do buttons, chips, cards and fields follow one shape rule (no more than two radii, one border weight, shadows only on floating elements)? (15-brand-art-direction/22 Monarch, 15-brand-art-direction/07 Hims)
7. Is the page still recognisable as Eleva with the logo covered, through its colour fields, its shapes or its photographic treatment? (15-brand-art-direction/09 Headspace, 15-brand-art-direction/05 Hims, 15-brand-art-direction/18 Ragged Edge)
8. Do consecutive sections use different layouts, with the deep colour field used once and the page ending in a branded closing block and footer? (15-brand-art-direction/16 Function, 15-brand-art-direction/17 Zaro, 15-brand-art-direction/21 Frontify, 15-brand-art-direction/23 metic., 15-brand-art-direction/24 Structured)
