# Sign in and sign up

> Generated from `references.json` by `docs/eleva-v3/design-audit/build.mjs`. Edit the JSON, then run the build.

Researcher seat: Onboarding and activation. Date: 2026-11-14. Before captures: `docs/eleva-v3/design-audit/captures/2026-11-14-before/05-auth/`.

The identifier-first flow is clean, short and on brand on desktop, and should be kept. Its weak points are at the edges: sign-up speaks only to members so a health professional sees no door, the phone layout drops every reassurance the desktop panel carries, and the two 'check your email' screens leave people waiting with nothing to do. The change that matters most is an expert variant of sign-up that carries intent into setup, with a compact trust line on mobile. References 12 and 13 (2026-11-21) prepare the return of people who had an account before the move: setting a new password with the account named, and a disconnected calendar with one button to reconnect.

## Keep

- Identifier-first is done properly: one email field or Google first, then a second step that shows the address as a chip with 'Change' (02-login-step-two, 04-signup-step-two). Keep this structure.
- The desktop brand panel has real presence: deep teal, the Lora headline with a rotating care area, and concrete points such as 'Online, by phone or in person'.
- The Google button follows Google's branding and sits first; a passkey option and 'Email me a sign-in link instead' are both present without crowding the screen.
- The magic-link screen states the expiry in plain words ('It works for 5 minutes') and offers 'Use a different email'.
- Copy is short, warm and in second person ('Almost there. Tell us your name and choose a password.').
- Password fields have a show/hide control, and the resend button counts down instead of failing silently.

## Judging criteria

A review panel scores a new design for this surface against these, with the references open.

1. Can a health professional tell from the sign-up and sign-in screens that she can join as an expert, and does choosing that path change the headline and carry her into practice setup? (05-auth/04, 05-auth/05)
2. On a 390 px phone, does the first step show at least one line of what Eleva is and the form's primary button above the keyboard? (05-auth/10, 05-auth/11)
3. Does the brand panel show a person (an expert or a member's words), not only a gradient and feature ticks? (05-auth/01, 05-auth/02)
4. On every 'check your email' screen, is the address on its own line with a way to change it, and is the primary action a button that opens the inbox? (05-auth/07, 05-auth/09)
5. Does the waiting screen say who the email is from and what to do if it does not arrive, with a link to help? (05-auth/08)
6. Can a new member create an account without inventing a password, and does a returning member see which method she used last? (05-auth/09, 05-auth/06)
7. Are a language menu (EN, PT, ES), a help link and Terms and Privacy reachable from every auth screen, on desktop and mobile? (05-auth/06, 05-auth/05)
8. Does a person returning after the move see why she is asked to set a new password, which account it is for and what happens next, and is a calendar that was disconnected shown as 'Disconnected' with one button to reconnect? (05-auth/12, 05-auth/13)
9. When signing in is held because of too many tries, does the message say how long to wait as a number from the answer, without blaming the person, say that another way of signing in still works, and stay one fixed message that is announced once? (05-auth/14, 05-auth/15, 05-auth/16)
