# How Clinic Bookings Are Paid

Your clinic pays Eleva a subscription, not a fee on each booking.

## What Your Clinic Pays

| Item            | Cost                                      |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Per-booking fee | None on bookings made through your clinic |
| Subscription    | Per seat, under the terms of your plan    |

## Independent Experts

The 15% service fee applies to individual experts. It doesn't apply to bookings made through your clinic.

## Paying Your Experts

How you share booking income with your experts is up to your clinic and your experts. Agree it with them before they join.
