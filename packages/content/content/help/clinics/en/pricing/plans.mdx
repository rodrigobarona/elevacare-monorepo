# Clinic Plans

A clinic plan has a monthly base price and a monthly price per seat. Eleva charges no fee on bookings made through your clinic.

| Plan       | Base price | Per seat  | Seats |
| ---------- | ---------- | --------- | ----- |
| Starter    | €99/month  | €39/month | 1–5   |
| Growth     | €199/month | €29/month | 6–20  |
| Enterprise | Custom     | Custom    | 20+   |

To start a plan, [contact us](/contact?partner=true).
