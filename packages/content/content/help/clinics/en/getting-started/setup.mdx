---
title: Create your clinic
description: Set up a Team for your clinic on Eleva in a few short steps.
---

You can create a clinic from any Eleva account. You become its admin, and Eleva opens it for you as soon as it's ready.

## Before you start

- **An Eleva account.** New to Eleva? Create one with your email, or select **Continue with Google**.
- **Your clinic's name.** Use the name your experts and members know you by. It needs at least 2 characters.

## Create your clinic

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Sign in

    Sign in to Eleva with the account you'll use to run the clinic.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Start a new clinic

    1. Open your account menu (your photo or initials) and choose **Account settings**.
    2. In the side menu, select **Workspaces**.
    3. Select the button at the top of the page to create a new one.
    4. Choose **Team**, then select **Continue**.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Name your clinic

    Type your clinic's name and confirm.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Open your clinic

    Eleva creates your clinic and takes you straight to its dashboard.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip" title="Other ways in">
  On the public site, open [For clinics](https://eleva.care/for-clinics) and
  select **Create your account**. Once you're signed in, you go straight to
  naming your clinic. If you already work on Eleva, you can also start a new
  clinic from the switcher at the top of the side menu.
</Callout>

## After you create it

- **You're the clinic's admin.** Your clinic appears under **Workspaces** in your account, marked **Admin**.
- **Your clinic's inbox.** Select the bell at the top of the page to see your clinic's alerts.
- **Moving between places.** Open your account menu: your clinic is listed with the label **Clinic**, and **Your Space** takes you back to your own care.
