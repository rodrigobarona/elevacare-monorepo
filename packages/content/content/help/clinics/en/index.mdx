A clinic on Eleva is a **Team**: one organization where licensed health experts work together, on the same platform members already use to find and book care.

You can set up your clinic yourself, in a few minutes. It lives in the same account as your personal Space and your own practice, if you have one, so you move between them without signing in again.

## What your clinic gives you today

- **A dashboard of its own.** Your clinic has its own home, kept apart from your Space and your practice.
- **An inbox of its own.** Eleva keeps your clinic's alerts in one place. The bell at the top of the page tells you when something new arrives.
- **One account for everything.** Move between your Space, your practice and your clinic from your account menu.
- **Clear terms.** The expert agreement sets out who provides the care, who invoices the member and how bookings made through a clinic are paid.
