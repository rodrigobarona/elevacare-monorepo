# Manage Your Experts

Your team of healthcare professionals is the heart of your clinic. This guide covers everything you need to know about managing experts on Eleva Care.

## Expert Types

<Tabs items={['In-House Experts', 'Network Experts']}>
  <Tab value="In-House Experts">
    Healthcare professionals employed by or contracted with your organization:

    - Work exclusively or primarily through your clinic
    - Use your clinic's branding
    - Sin tarifa por reserva de Eleva en las reservas realizadas a través de su clínica
    - Managed through your dashboard

  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Network Experts">
    Independent professionals from Eleva Care's expert network:

    - Work with multiple workspaces
    - Maintain their individual brand
    - Sin tarifa por reserva de Eleva en las reservas realizadas a través de su clínica
    - Can be featured on your clinic profile

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Adding Experts to Your clinic

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Invite Existing Eleva Care Experts">
    If the expert already has an Eleva Care profile:

    1. Go to **Team Management** → **Invite Expert**
    2. Enter their email address
    3. They receive an invitation to join your clinic
    4. Once accepted, their profile appears under your clinic

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Invite New Experts">
    For experts new to Eleva Care:

    1. Go to **Team Management** → **Invite New Expert**
    2. Enter their email and name
    3. They receive an invitation to create an Eleva Care profile
    4. After setup, they're automatically added to your clinic

  </Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Expert Dashboard

From your clinic dashboard, you can view:

### Expert Overview

| Metric                  | Description                               |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| **Total Experts**       | Number of experts in your clinic          |
| **Active Experts**      | Experts with bookings in the last 30 days |
| **Published Profiles**  | Experts with visible profiles             |
| **Pending Invitations** | Invitations not yet accepted              |

### Per-Expert Metrics

- **Bookings**: Total appointments scheduled
- **Revenue**: Gross revenue generated
- **Rating**: Average member rating
- **Availability**: Scheduled hours available

## Tarifas en las reservas de clínicas

Eleva no cobra tarifa por reserva en las reservas realizadas a través de una clínica. Su clínica paga una suscripción por puestos, en las condiciones de su plan.

Cómo reparte los ingresos de las reservas con sus especialistas es algo que deciden su clínica y sus especialistas. Acuérdelo con ellos antes de que se unan.

## Managing Expert Permissions

<Tabs items={['Admins Can', 'Admins Cannot']}>
  <Tab value="Admins Can">
    clinic administrators have these permissions:

    - View expert availability and bookings
    - Access aggregate analytics
    - Configure clinic-wide settings
    - Remove experts from the clinic

  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Admins Cannot">
    Administrators cannot:

    - Change expert's individual settings
    - Access expert's personal financial details
    - Modify expert's profile without permission
    - View bookings from other workspaces

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Removing Experts

To remove an expert from your clinic:

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Navigate to Team

    Go to **Team Management** in your dashboard.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Find Expert

    Locate the expert you want to remove.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Remove

    Click **Remove from clinic** and confirm.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
  **Note**: Removing an expert doesn't delete their Eleva Care profile. They can
  continue working independently or with other workspaces.
</Callout>

## Best Practices

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Onboarding">
    Best practices for onboarding new experts:

    1. **Expectativas claras**: Explique cómo paga a los especialistas y qué les ofrece
    2. **Support**: Help with profile optimization
    3. **Training**: Introduce them to the platform features
    4. **Communication**: Set up regular check-ins

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Retention">
    Keep your experts engaged and satisfied:

    1. **Pago justo**: Mantenga un pago a los especialistas claro y justo
    2. **member Volume**: Focus on driving bookings to your experts
    3. **Support**: Provide administrative and marketing support
    4. **Recognition**: Highlight top performers

  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
