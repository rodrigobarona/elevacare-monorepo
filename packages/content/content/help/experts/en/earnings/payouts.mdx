# Payouts & Earnings

Eleva Care uses Stripe Connect for secure, automated payouts. This guide explains how earnings work and how to maximize your income.

## How Payments Work

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### member Books

    Payment is collected at the time of booking.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Funds Held

    Eleva holds the payment through Stripe until your share is due.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Appointment Completes

    Your share becomes available on the later of 7 days after payment and 24 hours after the session ends. It's released at the next 04:00 Lisbon time.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Payout Processed

    Stripe then pays it out to your bank account on your account's payout schedule.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  This wait leaves time to resolve any disputes or issues. Eleva may hold a
  transfer while a dispute or refund is open.
</Callout>

## Service Fee

Eleva charges a service fee of **15% of the booking price**, VAT included where due. There are no other deductions. Eleva pays payment processing fees out of its fee.

### Example Calculation

| Booking Price | Service Fee (15%) | You Receive |
| ------------- | ----------------- | ----------- |
| €100          | €15               | €85         |

### Clinic Bookings

There is no per-booking fee on bookings made through a clinic. The clinic pays a per-seat subscription, under the terms of its plan.

### Paid Subscriptions

If you join a paid Eleva subscription that changes your fee, the terms shown when you join apply.

<Callout type="warning">

</Callout>

## Payout Schedule

Your share of a booking becomes available on the later of these two dates:

- 7 days after the member paid
- 24 hours after the session ends

It's released at the next 04:00 Lisbon time. Stripe then pays it out to your bank account on your account's payout schedule.

**Example**: A member pays on 1 March at 14:00 for a session that ends on 3 March at 10:00. Seven days after payment is 8 March at 14:00, which is the later date. Your share is released at 04:00 on 9 March.

<Callout type="info">
  Eleva staff review the first payout to your account before it's released, so
  it can take a little longer.
</Callout>

## Refunds & Cancellations

### member Cancellations

You choose a cancellation policy for each service: Flexible, Moderate or Strict. The policy shown at the time of booking applies to that booking.

| Policy   | 100% Refund             | 50% Refund                         | No Refund                 |
| -------- | ----------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Flexible | 24 hours or more before | —                                  | Less than 24 hours before |
| Moderate | 48 hours or more before | Between 24 and 48 hours before     | Less than 24 hours before |
| Strict   | 7 days or more before   | Between 48 hours and 7 days before | Less than 48 hours before |

Under every policy, a member gets a full refund if they cancel within 24 hours of booking and the session is at least 48 hours away.

The amount kept is split between you and Eleva in the same proportions as the booking. On a €100 booking:

| Member Refund | Member Gets Back | You Receive | Eleva Keeps |
| ------------- | ---------------- | ----------- | ----------- |
| 100%          | €100             | €0          | €0          |
| 50%           | €50              | €42.50      | €7.50       |
| None          | €0               | €85         | €15         |

If a member doesn't attend and hasn't cancelled, it counts as a cancellation at the session start time. The member gets no refund and you receive your share.

### Your Cancellations

If you have to cancel, or you don't attend:

- Tell Eleva at [support@eleva.care](mailto:support@eleva.care) as early as possible. Don't ask the member to cancel for you.
- The member receives 100% of the amount paid, including Eleva's fee, and Eleva offers them a new booking.
- You receive nothing for that booking. If the transfer was already made, the amount comes off your next transfers.

<Callout type="warning">
  The Eleva team handles these refunds. They aren't automatic. Repeated
  cancellations or no-shows may lead to your account being restricted or
  suspended.
</Callout>
