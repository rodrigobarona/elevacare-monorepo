---
title: Invoicing your members
description: You issue the invoice for each session with your own certified software. Here's how to set it up and keep track in Eleva.
---

Each session is agreed between you and your member, so you issue its invoice (or invoice-receipt) with invoicing software certified by the Portuguese Tax Authority (AT). You also decide the VAT treatment of the session, for example the exemption in Article 9 of the VAT Code, where it applies.

## Choose your invoicing method

In setup, at the **Invoicing** step:

1. Choose **Manual invoicing**.
2. Tick **I understand and accept this invoicing obligation**.
3. Choose **Acknowledge manual invoicing**.

You can see or change your method later in **Finance** → **Invoicing settings**.

## Keep track in Member invoices

**Finance** → **Member invoices** shows one row for each paid session, with its amount and status.

1. Issue the invoice to the member in your own invoicing software.
2. In **Member invoices**, find the session and choose **Mark as issued manually**, then confirm.

The status changes to **Issued manually**, so you can see at a glance which sessions still need an invoice.

## Monthly export

Under **Monthly export**, choose the month and **Prepare monthly export**. You get a file with that month's member invoices to help with your bookkeeping. It is not a certified tax file, so your official records stay in your invoicing software.
