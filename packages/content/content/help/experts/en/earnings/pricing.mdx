---
title: Prices and fees
description: How to set your prices, what Eleva keeps, and how promo codes affect your share.
---

You set your prices. The price you set is the final price members see and pay, in euros, with no extra charges after they confirm.

## Where you set prices

- **Service price**: in the service's **Basics** tab.
- **Price for one way to meet**: a delivery mode can have its own **Price override (€)**, for example for in-person sessions.
- **Private links**: a private link can carry its own price.

A price of 0 makes a free session, for example a short first call. Free sessions don't need payouts set up.

## What Eleva keeps

Eleva keeps a service fee of **15% of the booking price**, VAT included where due. There are no other deductions: Eleva pays the payment processing fees out of its fee.

| Booking price | Eleva's fee (15%) | You receive |
| ------------- | ----------------- | ----------- |
| €100          | €15               | €85         |

### Top Expert plan

Top Expert is an optional paid plan at €29 a month. Your fee is 8% instead of 15%, and your profile appears first when members sort by "Top Expert first", which is labelled as a paid plan. You can cancel at any time; the 15% fee applies again from the end of the paid month.

### Bookings through a clinic

There is no per-booking fee on bookings made through a clinic on Eleva. The clinic pays a subscription instead.

## Promo codes

Eleva promo codes are paid for by Eleva and never reduce what you receive. Your share is always calculated on your list price. For example, for a €100 service with a €20 code, the member pays €80 and you receive €85.

## Refunds

When a member cancels, your share follows the refund under the service's cancellation policy. [See what you receive](/experts/sessions/bookings)

## Setting a fair price

- Think about the whole time a session takes, including preparation and notes.
- Offer different services for different needs, such as a longer first consultation and shorter follow-ups.
- Present prices honestly: don't call a service free, discounted or on promotion in a misleading way.
