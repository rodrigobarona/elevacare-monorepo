Eleva takes care of bookings, payments and video sessions, so you can focus on care. You stay an independent professional: you choose your services, prices, hours and cancellation policy.

## What Eleva does for you

- **A public profile and booking page.** Members find you in the Eleva directory and book a session online, by phone or in person.
- **Payments.** Members pay when they book. Eleva sends your share to your bank through Stripe, our payments partner.
- **Video sessions.** Private calls in the browser, with your notes beside the video. Calls are not recorded.
- **Your calendar.** Weekly hours, days off and Google or Microsoft calendar sync, so you are never double-booked.
- **Session records.** Notes, private reports and session reports for your members, stored encrypted.

## From sign-up to your first booking

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Set up your practice

    Add your tax number, where you practise, your licence, and where your earnings go.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### We verify your licence

    We check your licence with your Ordem and your ERS registration, and email you when we're done.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Prepare your profile and services

    Add a photo and bio, create your services and set your hours. You can do this while we verify.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Members book you

    Once your licence is verified, members can find you and book your published services.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Fees in one line

Eleva keeps 15% of each booking, VAT included where due. There are no other deductions. [See prices and fees](/experts/earnings/pricing)
