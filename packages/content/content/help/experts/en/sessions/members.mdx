## Members

**Members** lists everyone who has booked a session with you, with their number of sessions and the next or last one. Members appear after their first booking.

Open a member to see their upcoming and past sessions, and the **Session report** for each one.

## Relationships

**Relationships**, under **Practice**, helps you see where each member stands and what to follow up on. Session history comes from your bookings.

- **Stage**: Lead, Active, Dormant or Churned.
- **Tags**: short labels you choose, such as "knee" or "sports".
- **Follow-ups**: choose **Schedule follow-up**, pick a **Due date** and add a note. Notes are private and encrypted. Due follow-ups appear at the top; choose **Done** when you've finished.
- **Add lead**: someone you're talking to who hasn't booked yet. Their email and phone are encrypted.

Stages and follow-ups are available for members with an Eleva account. For members who booked as guests, you still see their sessions.

<Callout>
  Use member details only to provide the care they booked and to meet your legal
  duties. Marketing messages need the member's consent.
</Callout>
