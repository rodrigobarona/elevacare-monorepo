---
title: Hours and calendars
description: Set your weekly hours and days off, and connect Google or Microsoft calendars to avoid double bookings.
---

Members can book you only inside your hours, and never over an event in a calendar you have connected.

## Set your weekly hours

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Open Schedule

    In the sidebar, under **Practice**, open **Schedule**. If nothing is set up yet, choose **Set up schedule**.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Check your time zone

    Your hours follow the **Timezone** at the top of the page. Members see your times in their own time zone.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Add your hours

    Under **Weekly hours**, choose **Add hours** for each day you work. A day can have more than one range, for example 09:00 to 13:00 and 14:00 to 18:00.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Save

    Choose **Save schedule**.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Days off and one-off changes

Under **Date exceptions**, choose **Add exception** and pick the date. Then choose **Block entire day**, or set different hours with **From** and **To**. Save the schedule.

Minimum notice, buffers and how far ahead members can book are set for each service, in its **Policies** tab. [See how](/experts/getting-started/services)

## Connect your calendars

Connect Google or Microsoft so Eleva checks your existing events and adds confirmed sessions to your calendar.

1. In the sidebar, under **Settings**, open **Connected calendars**.
2. Choose **Connect Google Calendar** or **Connect Microsoft Calendar** and allow access.
3. For each calendar, choose:
   - **Check for conflicts**: busy times in this calendar can't be booked.
   - **Write sessions here**: confirmed sessions are added to this calendar.

If a connection needs attention, a banner tells you. Reconnect it to keep conflict checks and new sessions working.

<Callout>
  Connecting a calendar is optional. Eleva is your calendar: members can still
  book, and you get an email invite for each session.
</Callout>

## Subscribe from another calendar app

In **Calendar**, find **ICS feed** and choose **Create feed**. Copy the link straight away, as it is shown only once, and add it to your calendar app:

- **Apple Calendar**: File → New Calendar Subscription
- **Google Calendar**: Settings → Add calendar → From URL
- **Outlook**: Add calendar → Subscribe from web

The feed is private and read-only. Choose **Rotate link** to get a new link, or **Revoke** to turn it off.
