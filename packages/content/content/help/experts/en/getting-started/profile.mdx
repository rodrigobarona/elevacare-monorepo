title: Your public profile
description: Add the photo, headline, bio and training that help members choose you.
---

Your public profile is what members see before they book. Open **Public profile** in the sidebar, save each section as you go, and choose **View public page** to see the result.

## Photo

Your photo is the largest element on your page. Members book people, not logos.

- Use JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB. A portrait (4:5) works best.
- Choose a recent photo of you alone, in natural light, against a plain background, dressed as you would for a session.
- Avoid group or cropped holiday photos, heavy filters, and logos or illustrations instead of you.

## Name and headline

- **Display name**: the name members know you by. You can include a title, for example "Dr. Ana Mota".
- **Headline**: one line on what you do and who you help, for example "Pelvic floor physiotherapist for pregnancy and postpartum".

## About you

Write your bio in your own language, then use **Translate from** your language to draft it in the others. Each draft is marked **AI draft — check it**: read it and fix anything before you save.

A bio that helps members choose covers:

1. What you do and who you help
2. How you work with members
3. Your training and experience
4. Why this work matters to you

<Callout type="tip">
  Write in the first person: "I help…" feels warmer than "She helps…".
</Callout>

## Training and credentials

Choose **Add credential** for each degree, specialisation or certification, with the institution and year. List only training you can show if asked.

## Social links

Choose **Add link** to add your website, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok or Facebook. Each link must start with `https://`.

## Keep it honest

Your profile and services count as health advertising under Portuguese law, and you are responsible for what they say. So:

- Don't promise a cure or results, or treatments without side effects.
- Don't make technical or scientific claims without evidence.
- Don't present a service as free, discounted or on promotion in a misleading way.
- Write clearly, using information the scientific community accepts.

If you use Eleva's writing or translation help, you remain responsible for the published text.

</Callout>
