## How It Works

<Tabs items={['What We Provide', 'What You Provide']}>
  <Tab value="What We Provide">
    **Platform Services**

    - Booking and scheduling system
    - Secure payment processing via Stripe
    - Video calls for your sessions
    - member management tools
    - Earnings dashboard and payouts

  </Tab>
  <Tab value="What You Provide">
    **Your Expertise**

    - Your professional expertise and credentials
    - Direct member care
    - Clinical documentation (if applicable)
    - Your professional judgment
    - Professional liability insurance

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout type="info">
  Eleva Care provides the technology platform. You, as an independent

</Callout>

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Complete Your Profile

    Add your photo, bio, credentials, and select your specializations.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Set Your Availability

    Configure your working hours and connect your calendar.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Create Services

    Define your consultation offerings with descriptions and pricing.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Connect Stripe

    Set up your payout account to receive earnings.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Verify Your Identity

    Complete identity and credential verification.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Publish Your Profile

    Make your profile visible to members and start accepting bookings.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tarifas

Eleva cobra una tarifa de servicio del 15 % del precio de la reserva, IVA incluido cuando proceda. En una reserva de 100 €, la tarifa es de 15 € y usted recibe 85 €. No hay otras deducciones.

[Más información sobre tarifas y pagos →](/experts/earnings/payouts)
