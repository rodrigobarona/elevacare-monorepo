# Pricing Strategy

Setting the right price is crucial for building a successful practice on Eleva Care. This guide helps you develop a pricing strategy that works.

## Pricing Principles

<Tabs items={['Value-Based', 'Market Position']}>
  <Tab value="Value-Based">
    **Value-Based Pricing**

    Price based on the value you provide, not just time spent:

    - Your expertise and credentials
    - member outcomes you deliver
    - Specialized training you hold
    - Years of experience

  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Market Position">
    **Market Positioning**

    Consider where you want to position yourself:

    | Position | Characteristics | Price Range |
    |----------|-----------------|-------------|
    | Premium | Extensive experience, specialized credentials | €120-200+ |
    | Mid-Range | Solid experience, good reviews | €80-120 |
    | Entry | Building experience, growing member base | €50-80 |

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Factors Affecting Price

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Credentials">
    Higher credentials justify higher prices:

    - Advanced degrees (PhD, Masters)
    - Specialized certifications
    - Published research
    - Speaking engagements

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Experience Level">
    | Experience | Typical Premium |
    |------------|-----------------|
    | 0-2 years | Base rate |
    | 3-5 years | +10-20% |
    | 6-10 years | +20-35% |
    | 10+ years | +35-50%+ |
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Specialization">
    Specialized services often command higher prices:

    - Rare expertise
    - Complex conditions
    - Innovative techniques
    - Exclusive training

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Market Demand">
    Adjust based on:

    - Your booking rate
    - Waitlist length
    - Competitor pricing
    - Seasonal patterns

  </Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Pricing Strategies

<Tabs items={['Tiered', 'Packages', 'Introductory']}>
  <Tab value="Tiered">
    **Tiered Pricing**

    Offer different price points for different needs:

    | Service | Duration | Price |
    |---------|----------|-------|
    | Initial Consultation | 60 min | €120 |
    | Follow-Up Standard | 45 min | €80 |
    | Follow-Up Brief | 30 min | €50 |
    | Extended Session | 90 min | €160 |

  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Packages">
    **Package Discounts** (Coming Soon)

    Encourage commitment with packages:

    | Package | Discount |
    |---------|----------|
    | 3 sessions | 10% off |
    | 5 sessions | 15% off |
    | 10 sessions | 20% off |

  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Introductory">
    **Introductory Pricing**

    Attract new members:

    - Limited-time lower rates
    - First consultation discount
    - Special promotional offers

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout type="info">
  Consider offering a slightly lower rate for your first few appointments to
  build reviews. Increase prices once you have a solid review base.
</Callout>

## Calculating Your Rate

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Determine Target Income

    Consider your desired monthly/annual income, hours you want to work, business expenses, and taxes.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Tenga en cuenta la tarifa de servicio

    | Su precio | Tarifa de servicio (15 %) | Usted recibe |
    |-----------|---------------------------|--------------|
    | 80 € | 12 € | 68 € |
    | 100 € | 15 € | 85 € |
    | 120 € | 18 € | 102 € |

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Calculate Hourly Equivalent

    Include time for the consultation itself, preparation, notes and documentation, and admin tasks.

    **Example**: 60-min consultation + 30 min prep/notes = 90 min total work time

    Si cobra 120 € y trabaja 90 minutos, recibe 102 € después de la tarifa de servicio. Eso supone una tarifa efectiva de 68 €/hora.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Adjusting Your Prices

<Tabs items={['When to Raise', 'When to Lower']}>
  <Tab value="When to Raise">
    **Raise Prices When:**

    - Consistently booked out
    - Long waitlist
    - Gained new credentials
    - Positive review growth
    - After 6-12 months of experience

    **How to Raise:**
    1. Give advance notice to existing members
    2. Update service prices
    3. Consider grandfathering existing members briefly
    4. Communicate value, not just price

  </Tab>
  <Tab value="When to Lower">
    **Lower Prices When:**

    - Low booking rate
    - New to the platform
    - Entering a competitive niche
    - Building your member base

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Competitor Research

### How to Research

1. Browse expert profiles in your specialty
2. Note their credentials and prices
3. Compare experience levels
4. Consider their review ratings

### What to Compare

| Factor               | Weight |
| -------------------- | ------ |
| Same specialty       | High   |
| Similar experience   | High   |
| Same region/language | Medium |
| Similar credentials  | Medium |

<Callout type="info">
  Don't just compete on price. Focus on your unique value proposition —
  expertise, approach, personality.
</Callout>

## Common Mistakes

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Pricing Too Low">
    - Undervalues your expertise - Attracts price-sensitive members - Hard to
    raise later - Lower perceived quality
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Pricing Too High Initially">
    - Few bookings to build reviews - Harder to establish presence - May need to
    lower (looks bad)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Ignoring Fees">
    - Price €100, think you'll earn €100 - Olvida contar con la tarifa de
    servicio del 15 %: recibe 85 € - End up with lower effective rate
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="One-Size-Fits-All">
    - Same price for all services - Missing opportunity for premium offerings -
    Not serving different needs
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
