Your Space keeps your sessions, payments, reports and privacy settings in one place.

## Sign in or create an account

1. Go to [eleva.care/login](https://eleva.care/login).
2. Select **Continue with Google**, or enter your email and select **Continue**.
3. Enter your password and select **Sign in**, or select **Email me a sign-in link instead**. The link works for 5 minutes.

You can also select **Sign in with a passkey**. Forgot your password? Select **Forgot password?** and we'll email you a reset link.

New to Eleva? Select **Create an account** on the sign-in page. Enter your email or continue with Google, add your name and a password of at least 8 characters, then open the verification link we email you.

<Callout>
  Booked without an account? Sign in with the same email, with a sign-in link or
  Google, and your booking is waiting in your Space.
</Callout>

## Find your way around

| Page     | What you'll find                                           |
| -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Home     | A quick look at your sessions and a link to find an expert |
| Sessions | Upcoming and past sessions: join, reschedule or cancel     |
| Payments | Your payments, receipts and refunds                        |
| Reports  | Reports your experts share with you after a session        |
| Settings | Profile, language, theme and notifications                 |
| Privacy  | Consents, a copy of your data and deleting your account    |

## Settings

In your Space, go to **Settings** to:

- update your name, time zone and profile photo (JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 2 MB);
- choose your language and theme: light, dark, or the same as your device;
- choose how Eleva reaches you (email, SMS or in-app) for each kind of message, and set quiet hours to pause non-urgent messages.

To keep your account secure, open the menu under your profile photo and select **Account settings**. There you can change your password, turn on two-factor authentication with an authenticator app, add a passkey, connect Google, and sign out of devices you don't recognise.

## Privacy and your data

In your Space, go to **Privacy** to:

- **See your consents.** Check what you agreed to and withdraw marketing, health data or AI consent. Health data consent stays in place while you have a confirmed session coming up.
- **Choose clinic record sharing.** If a clinic lets its experts share session records, you can opt out for that clinic.
- **Download your data.** Select **Request export**. We prepare a zip file, usually within a few minutes, and the download link works for 24 hours.
- **Delete your account.** Select **Schedule deletion**. Deletion runs after 14 days, and you can cancel it until then. New bookings stop as soon as you confirm; future and unpaid sessions are cancelled, and paid sessions are marked for a refund. Records linked to a session no longer carry your name or account, and are kept for the period the law requires.

You can change your cookie choices at any time from **Cookie preferences** in the site footer or in the menu under your profile photo. To learn more, read our [privacy notice](https://eleva.care/legal/privacy).
