## About Eleva

<Accordions>

<Accordion title="What does Eleva Care do?" id="what-is-eleva">

Eleva Care helps you find independent women's health experts, book and pay for sessions, get reminders and join video calls. Your expert provides your care and is responsible for it. Eleva hosts the booking and the call.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How does Eleva check experts?" id="expert-checks">

Before a profile goes live, our team checks the expert's professional licence number and their registration with their professional body. Experts must keep that registration valid. Each expert describes their own training and experience on their profile.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How are experts ordered in the directory?" id="ranking">

By default, experts are listed by name. Experts marked **Top Expert · paid plan** pay for a plan that shows them first when you choose to sort by "Top Expert first". Section 8 of our [terms of use](https://eleva.care/legal/terms) explains how we order experts.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does it cost anything to use Eleva?" id="cost">

Browsing and creating an account are free. You pay the price of each session, set by the expert, with no Eleva fee on top. Some experts also offer free sessions.

</Accordion>

</Accordions>

## Booking

<Accordions>

<Accordion title="Do I need an account to book?" id="account-needed">

No. You can book with just your name and email. We save the booking in a personal Space under that email, and you can sign in to it whenever you like. See [Your Space and account](/members/account).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I book for someone else?" id="book-for-others">

Bookings are for adults booking for themselves. When you book, you confirm that you are 18 or older and that the session is for you. To arrange care for someone under 18, or for another person, contact the expert directly.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I tell the expert something before the session?" id="note-for-expert">

Yes. When you book, use **Anything you want the expert to know**. Your expert sees it with your session record.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I use my health insurance?" id="insurance">

If your insurance reimburses private sessions, ask your insurer what they need. Your expert issues the invoice for your session, in your name if you add your NIF when you book.

</Accordion>

</Accordions>

## Sessions

<Accordions>

<Accordion title="Do I need to install an app?" id="app">

No. Eleva works in your browser on a phone, tablet or computer. For video sessions, use an up-to-date browser such as Chrome, Safari, Firefox or Edge.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My video or sound isn't working. What can I do?" id="video-issues">

1. Check that your browser can use your camera and microphone.
2. Refresh the page and join again.
3. Try another browser, or move closer to your Wi-Fi router.
4. Still stuck? Write to [support@eleva.care](mailto:support@eleva.care) and tell us which device and browser you're using.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Are sessions recorded?" id="recording">

No. Your video call is private and is not recorded. If you allow it when you book, your session is transcribed live to help your expert keep their notes. You can withdraw that choice at any time under **Privacy** in your Space.

</Accordion>

</Accordions>

## Privacy

<Accordions>

<Accordion title="Is my health information safe?" id="data-safety">

Eleva is based in the European Union and protects your data under EU data protection law (GDPR). Session records are encrypted, and only the people who need them can open them. Read our [privacy notice](https://eleva.care/legal/privacy) for the details.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I get a copy of my data or delete my account?" id="data-rights">

In your Space, go to **Privacy**. You can download your data or schedule your account's deletion there. See [Your Space and account](/members/account).

</Accordion>

</Accordions>
