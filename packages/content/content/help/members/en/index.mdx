Eleva Care connects you with independent women's health experts. You can meet them by video, by phone or in person, in English, Portuguese or Spanish.

## How it works

1. **Find an expert.** Browse by area of care and read each profile: services, training, prices and cancellation terms.
2. **Book a time.** Choose a service and a time, add your name and email, then pay or confirm a free session. You don't need an account.
3. **Meet your expert.** Join a video session from the link in your email, take your expert's phone call, or meet them at their practice.

## Your Space

Your Space is your personal place on Eleva. It keeps your sessions, payments, reports and privacy settings together. If you book without an account, we set up a Space for you with the email you used. Sign in with that email whenever you want to open it.

<Callout type="warn">
  Eleva is not for emergencies. If you need urgent help, call 112.
</Callout>
