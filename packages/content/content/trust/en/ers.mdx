## Eleva's role

Eleva.care is an **intermediary**. It connects members with **independent health professionals** and provides booking and the video call, and collects payment on the expert's behalf. Eleva **does not provide healthcare** and does not replace emergency services (112).

The contract for the session is made between the member and the expert, who provides the care, is responsible for it and issues the invoice.

## Only regulated, verified professionals

- Only professionals in **regulated health professions**, with active registration with their professional body (Ordem), may offer paid sessions.
- Before a profile is published, Eleva **checks the professional licence number (cédula profissional) and the status of the registration** with the competent professional body.
- The expert must keep their registration valid and tell Eleva of any suspension or restriction. If the registration can no longer be confirmed, the profile stops accepting bookings.
- Verification confirms professional registration. It is not an assessment of clinical quality.

## The expert's ERS registration

Healthcare establishments, including those that provide remote care (telemedicine), must register in advance with the Entidade Reguladora da Saúde (Articles 4 and 26 of the ERS Statutes, approved by Decree-Law 126/2014). Each expert is responsible for their own ERS registration and for the related duties. Where registration is required, the expert must hold it and display the number on their profile and in their health advertising.

## Health advertising

The profiles and services published on Eleva are health advertising, subject to Decree-Law 238/2015 and ERS Regulation 1058/2016:

- the profile must identify the expert, their licence number and the professional body that issued it, their qualifications, their ERS registration number (where applicable) and, for in-person services, the address;
- guarantees of cure or results, claims without scientific evidence, encouragement of unnecessary care and misleading discounts are not allowed;
- Eleva may ask for corrections or remove content that does not comply with these rules, and explains the reason to the expert.

## Remote care

The expert decides whether an online session is appropriate and when the member should seek in-person or emergency care.
