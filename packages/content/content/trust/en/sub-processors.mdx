## Overview

Eleva uses vetted vendors under Article 28 GDPR contracts. This page lists the vendors that handle personal data on our instructions. Transfer mechanisms combine **EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)** in each vendor DPA with **EU–US Data Privacy Framework (DPF)** certification where the vendor maintains it (checked periodically).

Neon, Vercel, Cloudflare, Daily, Deepgram and OpenAI are the vendors that can handle session records. The other vendors do not receive them.
