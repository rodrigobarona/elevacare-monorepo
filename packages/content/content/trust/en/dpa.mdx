# Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

_Effective Date: December 31, 2025_

> **Quick Summary**
>
> - **What this page is about:** How Eleva.care processes personal data on behalf of users and experts.
> - **Eleva.care is an intermediary marketplace and does not provide healthcare; independent, licensed experts provide clinical care.**
> - **For platform data** (accounts, bookings, payments): Eleva.care is the Data Controller.
> - **For clinical notes/files** created by experts: the expert is the Controller; Eleva.care stores them securely.
> - **Session records are stored in the EU** (Frankfurt), protected by encryption and strict access controls. Section 12 shows where each vendor processes data, including outside the EU.
> - **Questions?** Contact `privacy@eleva.care` (Eleva is appointing an external data protection officer).

This DPA explains how Eleva.care handles personal data—clearly and simply.

Eleva.care is an intermediary marketplace that connects members with independent health professionals, who deliver the sessions. Eleva.care does not provide healthcare. Eleva.care's regulatory status under applicable Portuguese and EU health regulations is distinct from its GDPR data protection roles, and Eleva.care will comply with applicable health regulations.

## 1) Who does what

- Eleva.care is an intermediary marketplace: independent, licensed professionals provide the care, and Eleva.care does not provide healthcare.
- Two roles:
  - Controller (platform operations: accounts, bookings, payments, support)
  - Processor (clinical notes/files that experts create: we store securely on their instructions)

## 2) When we are Controller (platform)

- We use trusted processors to run the platform. Section 12 lists them with their regions and safeguards.
  - Sign-in and calendar sync: Google and Microsoft, when the person chooses them (independent controllers)
  - Payments: Stripe (PCI DSS L1), which also acts as an independent controller for its own legal obligations
- We require DPAs/SCCs, security controls, and EU data residency where available.

## 3) When we are Processor (for experts)

- Experts are Controllers of clinical data; we are their Processor.
- We act only on documented instructions, keep data encrypted (at rest and in transit), and maintain RBAC and audit logs.
- Session records are stored in Neon (Frankfurt). Section 12 lists every sub-processor that can handle them. We notify before changes.

## 4) Security at a glance

- TLS 1.3 in transit; AES‑256‑GCM at rest (clinical data)
- Least privilege/RBAC; MFA available; audit logging; encrypted backups; DR plans
- 24‑hour breach notice SLA to Controllers for clinical data

## 5) International transfers

- We prioritize EU data residency. Where a vendor or its staff outside the EEA may access data, we rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (DPF) where the vendor is certified and on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), plus supplementary measures (encryption, key controls, access logging, challenge overbroad requests). Section 12 shows the safeguards for each vendor.

## 6) Retention and deletion

- Platform data: kept only as long as needed or required by law
- Session records (clinical data): kept by default for 7 years after the last session. On instruction/termination, we export and delete following a short grace period and backup window.

## 7) Data subject rights

- Platform (Controller): We handle access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, and objection requests.
- Clinical (Processor): We provide tools so experts can fulfill member requests.

## 8) Expert responsibilities (as Controller)

- Obtain informed consent where needed; ensure lawful basis (e.g., GDPR Art. 9(2)(h))
- Set retention; respond to rights requests; maintain accuracy; carry malpractice insurance

## 9) Our responsibilities (as Processor)

- Follow instructions; keep data secure; assist with rights requests and DPIAs; notify breaches without undue delay; return/delete data on request; limit transfers; keep confidentiality
- **Unlawful instructions:** we tell the expert immediately if we believe an instruction infringes the GDPR or other EU or Member State data protection law.
- **Audits:** we make available all the information needed to demonstrate compliance with Article 28 GDPR, and we allow for and contribute to audits, including inspections, by the expert or an auditor they appoint. Send requests to `privacy@eleva.care` with reasonable notice. Audits must protect the confidentiality and security of other experts' and members' data.
- **Sub-processors:** we use a sub-processor only under a written contract that imposes the same data protection obligations as this DPA, including sufficient guarantees of appropriate technical and organisational measures. We remain fully liable to the expert for each sub-processor's performance of those obligations.

## 10) Liability

- Eleva.care: responsible for the operation, security, and organizational/technical/administrative compliance of the platform as described.
- Experts: liable for clinical decisions, member outcomes, and compliance as Controllers of clinical data.

## 11) Updates, law, and disputes

- We may update this DPA with notice for material changes
- Governing law: Portugal; venue: courts of Portugal
