## 1. Definitions

- **Platform:** the Eleva.care websites and apps and the infrastructure that supports them.
- **Member:** a person who uses Eleva to find and book sessions with experts.
- **Expert:** an independent health professional in a regulated profession, whose active registration with their professional body (Ordem) Eleva has verified.
- **Clinic:** a healthcare organisation that offers sessions with its professionals through Eleva.
- **Session:** the health service that an expert provides to a member, online or in person.

## 2. Eleva's role

- Eleva is an **intermediary**. It helps you find experts, book and pay for sessions, receive reminders and join the video call.
- Eleva **does not provide healthcare**. It does not employ the experts and does not direct or supervise their clinical decisions.
- The contract for the session is made **directly between you and the expert** (or the clinic). The expert is the provider of the care and is responsible for it.
- The expert issues you the **invoice for the session**. Eleva does not invoice the session and does not charge VAT on it.

## 3. Verified experts

- Only health professionals in regulated professions may offer paid sessions. Before a profile is published, Eleva checks the expert's professional licence number (cédula profissional) and the status of their registration with the competent professional body.
- Experts must keep their registration valid and tell us of any change. If Eleva can no longer confirm the registration, the profile stops accepting bookings.
- Each expert is responsible for the content of their profile and for their own registration with the Entidade Reguladora da Saúde (ERS), including showing the registration number on their profile where applicable.
- Verification confirms professional registration. It is not an assessment of the clinical quality of the care.

## 4. Your account

- To create an account or book a session, you must be 18 or older and have legal capacity to enter into contracts.
- If you book without an account, we create a member account in your name after the booking is confirmed, so you can manage it. You can use it, or ask us to delete it. See the [Privacy notice](/legal/privacy).
- Give accurate information, keep your access secure and tell us about any unauthorised use at [support@eleva.care](mailto:support@eleva.care).

## 5. Bookings

- **Bookings are for adults.** You book sessions for yourself. Before you confirm a booking, you tick the box "I am 18 or older and I am booking this session for myself, as set out in the booking terms". We keep that confirmation with the booking.
- To arrange care for someone under 18, or for another person, contact the expert directly.
- If an expert or Eleva finds that a session is for someone under 18 or for someone other than the person who booked, the expert may decline to start it. We then cancel the booking and refund what you paid.
- Before you confirm, you see the service, duration, final price, mode and the cancellation policy that applies.
- During payment, the time slot is held for 5 minutes (10 minutes while an MB WAY payment is being processed).
- After confirmation, you receive an email with the details and, for online sessions, the link to join. You can join from 15 minutes before the start until 30 minutes after the end.

## 6. Prices, payments and invoices

- Prices are shown in euros and are final. There are no extra charges after confirmation.
- Payment is made through Stripe Checkout. We accept card (including Apple Pay and Google Pay), Link and MB WAY.
- Eleva collects the payment **on the expert's behalf** and transfers the expert's share to them. Paying Eleva counts as paying the expert: once your payment is confirmed, you owe the expert nothing more for that session.
- You may choose to give your NIF. Eleva collects it and sends it to the expert so that they can issue the invoice in your name.
- **Promo codes.** Eleva may offer promo codes, on the conditions stated for each campaign. Eleva covers the discount, and it does not change what the expert receives.
- The details are in the [Payment policies](/legal/payments).

## 7. Cancellations, no-shows and refunds

- **Cancellation by you:** the refund follows the service's policy (Flexible, Moderate or Strict) shown at the time of booking, including the 24-hour grace period. The full rules are in the [Payment policies](/legal/payments).
- **Your no-show:** if you do not attend and have not cancelled, the no-show is treated as a cancellation at the session start time. Under all three policies, this means there is no refund.
- **Expert cancellation or no-show:** if the expert cancels or does not attend, you receive **a full refund of what you paid**, and we offer you a new booking.
- If the expert does not attend, tell us at [support@eleva.care](mailto:support@eleva.care). For online sessions, we confirm attendance with the video call join logs.

## 8. How we rank experts

Experts appear in listings in this order:

- the filters you choose (category and language) decide who appears;
- by default, experts are listed in alphabetical order of their name;
- you can also sort by **lowest price**, from the cheapest service to the most expensive, or by **Top Expert first**: experts with the paid **Top Expert** subscription appear first, then the others in alphabetical order.

Top Expert is marked as a paid plan wherever it appears. It is the only form of payment that influences the order. Only experts whose professional registration Eleva has verified appear.
