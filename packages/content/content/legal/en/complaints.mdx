## Who we are

The **Eleva.care** platform is operated by **BÚZIOS E TARTARUGAS, LDA** (NIPC 515001708), registered office at Rua Gil Vicente 2, 2775-198 Parede, Portugal.

## How to contact us

For questions about your account, a booking, or how the platform works, email [support@eleva.care](mailto:support@eleva.care).

For contractual or consumer complaints about Eleva as the platform provider, email [legal@eleva.care](mailto:legal@eleva.care). Include your name, account email (if any), the date of the events, and what you are asking for. We reply within the applicable legal time limits.

To exercise data protection rights, contact [privacy@eleva.care](mailto:privacy@eleva.care). You may also complain to the Portuguese Data Protection Authority (CNPD), Av. D. Carlos I, 134, 1.º, 1200-651 Lisbon, [www.cnpd.pt](https://www.cnpd.pt).

## Electronic complaints book (Livro de Reclamações)

Under Portuguese Decree-Law 156/2005, you can use the electronic complaints book at [www.livroreclamacoes.pt](https://www.livroreclamacoes.pt/inicio) to record a complaint about goods or services you bought from us.

## Healthcare services delivered by experts

Eleva **does not provide clinical care**. Independent experts you book through the platform are responsible for the care they deliver. Complaints about the clinical content of a session should be raised with the expert first. If your complaint also concerns platform conduct (payments, access, cancellations, or data Eleva processes as intermediary), use the contacts above.

## Consumer alternative dispute resolution (RAL)

Eleva **has not joined** a consumer arbitration centre. Using RAL is **voluntary** for you and for us.

Under Article 18 of Law 144/2015, the following bodies are among those available for consumer disputes relating to contracts with us:

- **CACCL — Centro de Arbitragem de Conflitos de Consumo de Lisboa**, Rua dos Douradores, 116, 2.º, 1100-207 Lisbon, [www.centroarbitragemlisboa.pt](https://www.centroarbitragemlisboa.pt). Territorial scope: Lisbon Metropolitan Area, including Cascais. Value limit: €5,000.
- **CNIACC — Centro Nacional de Informação e Arbitragem de Conflitos de Consumo**, Rua D. Afonso Henriques, 1, 4700-030 Braga, [www.cniacc.pt](https://www.cniacc.pt). National scope, supplementary to regional centres. No value limit.

Updated list of centres supported by the Portuguese Ministry of Justice: [gov.pt — consumer arbitration](https://www.gov.pt/servicos/realizar-uma-arbitragem-de-conflitos-de-consumo-atraves-dos-centros-de-arbitragem-apoiados-pelo-ministerio-da-justica).

## EU online dispute resolution platform

The European ODR platform for online dispute resolution **closed on 20 July 2025**. Use the contacts and complaints book on this page instead.

## Digital Services Act (DSA)

If you use Eleva as a member or visitor and want to report illegal content on the platform under Regulation (EU) 2022/2065, send a notice to [legal@eleva.care](mailto:legal@eleva.care) with the URL, a description of the content, and the legal basis you rely on.
