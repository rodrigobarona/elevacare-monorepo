## 1. Who the controller is

The controller of the platform's data is **BÚZIOS E TARTARUGAS - LDA** ("Eleva"), NIPC 515001708, Rua Gil Vicente 2, 2775-198 Parede, Portugal.

- **Data protection contact:** [privacy@eleva.care](mailto:privacy@eleva.care).
- **Data protection officer (DPO):** Eleva is appointing an external DPO. When the appointment is complete, we will publish their contact details here and notify them to the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD). Until then, and afterwards too, you can use [privacy@eleva.care](mailto:privacy@eleva.care).

## 2. Our role

- Eleva is an intermediary: it does not provide healthcare. The experts you choose are independent professionals.
- Eleva is the **controller** of account, booking, payment, communications and support data.
- The expert is the **controller** of the clinical content of your relationship with them (intake forms, notes, transcripts and reports). Eleva stores that content as a **processor**, on the expert's instructions. See the [Health data notice](/legal/health-data) and the [Data processing agreement](/trust/dpa).

## 3. Data we process

- **Account:** name, email, phone number (if you give it, for example for SMS reminders), language and preferences.
- **Bookings:** expert, service, date, mode, cancellation policy applied, cancellations and refunds.
- **Payments:** amount, payment and refund status, promo code used and NIF, if you give it. Card details are handled by Stripe; Eleva does not store them.
- **Online sessions:** video call join and leave logs. Session recording is turned off.
- **Session records (health data):** what you share before the session, the expert's notes, the report and, only if you turn it on, the live transcript. They are encrypted at the application layer: the Eleva team cannot read them.
- **Communications:** booking and reminder emails and SMS, and support requests.
- **Technical data:** IP address, device, security logs and errors.
- **Experts:** identity, professional licence number (cédula profissional) and professional body (Ordem), ERS registration, verification and payment data collected by Stripe, and the tax data required by DAC7.
- **Consents:** what you accepted, when, and which version of the document.

## 4. Purposes and legal bases

| Purpose                                                                                                                    | Legal basis                                                                            |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Creating and managing your account, booking sessions, sending confirmations and reminders, giving access to the video call | Performance of the contract (GDPR, art. 6(1)(b))                                       |
| Collecting, refunding and transferring payments; sending your NIF to the expert for the invoice                            | Performance of the contract; legal obligation (art. 6(1)(c))                           |
| Keeping accounting and tax records                                                                                         | Legal obligation                                                                       |
| Storing session records on the expert's instructions                                                                       | The expert's basis: provision of healthcare (art. 9(2)(h), and Law 58/2019, art. 29)   |
| Live session transcription                                                                                                 | Your explicit consent (art. 9(2)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time               |
| Proving attendance at a session and responding to payment disputes                                                         | Legitimate interest (art. 6(1)(f)); establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims |
| Security, fraud and abuse prevention                                                                                       | Legitimate interest                                                                    |
| Verifying experts and reporting their income to the AT (DAC7)                                                              | Legal obligation                                                                       |
| Newsletter and marketing communications                                                                                    | Consent, which you can withdraw at any time                                            |

We do not use health data for advertising.

## 5. Recipients

- **The expert** you book receives the data needed for the session: your name, contact details, booking details, what you share before the session and your NIF, if you give it.
- **Processors** that help us operate the platform (hosting, database, email, SMS, video, payments and error monitoring), under a contract in line with art. 28 GDPR. The current list is in [Sub-processors](/trust/sub-processors).
- **Stripe** also acts as an independent controller for its own legal obligations, for example identity verification and fraud prevention.
- **Google or Microsoft**, if you choose to sign in with those accounts, act as independent controllers.
- **The Autoridade Tributária e Aduaneira**, for the experts' tax data required by DAC7.
- **Authorities and courts**, when the law requires it.

## 6. International transfers

Our data is hosted in the European Union (Frankfurt), unless the sub-processor list states otherwise. Some providers, or their support teams, access data from the United States. In those cases, the transfer is based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. See the details in [Sub-processors](/trust/sub-processors).

## 7. Retention

## 8. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and you can withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting processing already carried out.

- Write to [privacy@eleva.care](mailto:privacy@eleva.care). We reply within one month, which can be extended by two further months for complex requests, in which case we tell you.
- For clinical content, for which the expert is the controller, we may forward your request to the expert and help them respond.
- You can complain to the CNPD, Av. D. Carlos I, 134, 1.º, 1200-651 Lisbon, [www.cnpd.pt](https://www.cnpd.pt).

## 9. Security

Session records are encrypted at the application layer, health files are kept in private storage, access is limited to the roles that need it and video recording is turned off. See the [Security](/trust/security) page.

## 10. Artificial intelligence and cookies

- Eleva's AI tools help experts write and translate the public text of their profile and do not receive member data or health data. See the [AI processing notice](/legal/ai-processing).
- We do not use analytics or advertising cookies. See the [Cookie policy](/legal/cookies).

## 11. Minors

Accounts are intended for people aged 18 or over.
